Note: I changed the alphas of the points to fade when further from the correlation, according to the findings of Strain et al. 

The correlation between housing prices and construction is 0.12, which is counter to what I would expect if supply-and-demand were to hold true (more construction leads to more supply which decreases price). There are some issues with this plot, namely that sampling is not independent (multiple MSAs show up in different years). Also, construction likely has some sort of lagged effect, perhaps into the next month or the next year. I want to create a plot or edit the data to reflect this lag (and also make independence valid) by looking at the change in housing prices (perhaps relative to the national trend?) following construction.
